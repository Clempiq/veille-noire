# Illustrations V1.5

Création avec l’outil intégré `image_gen`, sans API ni clé utilisateur. Trois atlas originaux, inspectés visuellement puis convertis en JPEG pour réduire leur poids total à environ 1,6 Mo. Les PNG originaux sont conservés dans le dossier de génération Codex. Aucun actif d’une franchise existante n’a été utilisé.

Fichiers utilisés par le jeu :

- `assets/terrain-atlas-v15.jpg` : huit textures de terrain, grille 4 × 2.
- `assets/awakened-sheet-v15.jpg` : quatre portraits, grille 2 × 2.
- `assets/equipment-atlas-v15.jpg` : seize équipements, grille 4 × 4.

## Prompts finaux

### Terrain

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset for an original French survival board game called Veille Noire, a drowned industrial harbor in 2091. Create ONE rectangular terrain texture atlas, exactly 4 equal columns by 2 equal rows, eight square tiles with NO gutters, NO borders, NO text, NO labels, NO characters or game tokens. True orthographic directly top-down bird's-eye view, detailed hand-painted premium tabletop game art, realistic materials and tiny environmental storytelling, desaturated petrol teal, moss, rust, warm ivory accents, readable midtones, atmospheric but not too dark. Each square is a floor section with furnishings near corners and generous open center to place tokens. No dividing walls, no roofs, no perspective/isometric. Exact cell order left to right: top row 1 rain-wet old stone harbor pavement with rope and puddles, 2 abandoned mechanical workshop concrete floor with workbench and tools at top edge, 3 radio station tiled floor with radio desk cables in corner, 4 abandoned infirmary faded ivory floor with one medical cot and cabinet at edge. Bottom row: 5 wooden warehouse floor with weathered wooden crates at corner, 6 industrial pump room metal grating floor with pipes and wheel valve near edge, 7 flooded archive room dark wooden parquet with desk books and papers at edge, 8 stone harbor pavement with a realistic leafy tree canopy rooted at corner and fallen leaves. Rich tactile brushwork and convincing overhead shadows. These are decorative walkable terrain squares, not an overall map. No logos, no interface. Landscape aspect ratio 2:1.

### Éveillés

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a square 2 by 2 character portrait atlas for original French tactical survival game Veille Noire, flooded industrial harbor in 2091. Exactly four equal square portraits, no gutters, no borders, no text, no symbols, no logos. Cinematic painterly realistic character art, detailed faces and worn practical clothing, charcoal teal background rainy ruined harbor, waist/chest up centered face in each quadrant, consistent camera scale and muted lighting matching a serious premium tabletop survival game. Top left: Maëlys Orne, woman around 35 with dark olive skin, long black hair streaked with white, worn petrol raincoat, bioluminescent cyan hands gathering a small spectral tide flame, tide mage. Top right: Orso Venn, sturdy older man with brown skin, shaved head silver beard, weathered bronze armor over harbor diving suit, amber protective magic around one bracer, guardian. Bottom left: Silène Dorne, agile androgynous young adult with pale freckled skin, short auburn hair, dark hood down, twin steel daggers and a faint violet mist around shoulders, mist duelist. Bottom right: Ysée Lorn, older East Asian woman with long tied gray hair, white and dark teal medical coat, small luminous green marine crystal in palms, healer of the tides. Fully original designs, subtly supernatural but grounded in a ruined maritime city, no copied franchise designs. Faces large and clearly distinct for small game tokens.

### Équipements

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE square equipment sprite atlas, exactly FOUR equal columns by FOUR equal rows, 16 isolated objects, one centered object per cell, consistent near-black dark petrol background across whole atlas, no frames no gutters no labels no text. Premium realistic painterly tabletop survival game inventory art, weathered maritime industrial objects, directional overhead studio lighting, readable silhouettes, each object fits within its square with 12 percent empty margin, no characters. Exact cell order left to right top to bottom. Row 1: heavy adjustable metal wrench; worn harbor fire axe with wood handle; chunky orange marine flare pistol; elegant wooden recurve bow. Row 2: short pump-action shotgun; long bolt-action rifle with scope; pneumatic harpoon gun with steel spear; curved diver's combat knife. Row 3: copper staff holding glowing cyan sea crystal; legendary obsidian longsword with cyan illuminated blade edge; legendary industrial railgun with glowing amber power coils; legendary open brass reliquary containing floating violet crystal. Row 4: old canvas medical bag with white cross; three brass cartridges next to small metal ammo box; red marine flare with little red smoke; compact cylindrical thermal grenade. Fully original post-apocalyptic harbor aesthetic, beautiful tactile metal cloth and wood, detailed but instantly identifiable small icons.
